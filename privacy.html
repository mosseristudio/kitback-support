Privacy Policy – Kitback

Last updated: October 2026

Kitback is an app for tracking production gear: what went out to a shoot, what came back, and what is missing. It is built so that we know nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't collect, send or store any data with us: no account or sign-up, no server, no ads, no analytics or usage measurement, and no tracking. The app doesn't connect to the internet at all.

What is stored, and where

Your gear list and scan history are stored only on your device: item names, categories, serial numbers, values and notes you enter, the codes assigned to items, and the log of what was scanned, when, and on which trip.

If your phone's backup is on (iCloud or Google), this data is normally included in it, with the rest of your phone. That backup is yours and lives in your own account, not with us.

We can't see this information, recover it or delete it for you, because it never reaches us.

Permissions

Camera – to scan the QR labels on your gear. The image is processed on the device in real time and is never saved, uploaded or shared. If you decline, you can type codes by hand and the app keeps working.

Sharing

When you choose to export – a label sheet (PDF), a full backup, an insurance spreadsheet or a trip summary – the file is created on your device and handed to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice, and we are not involved.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Settings has Delete all data. Before deleting, the app shows how many items and trips will be removed and offers to export a backup first. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date.
