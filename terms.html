Terms of Use – Kitback

Last updated: October 2026

What the app does

Kitback keeps a list of your gear on your device, records what you scan on the way out and on the way back, and shows what didn't come back. There is no server and no account: everything stays on your phone.

What the report is based on

The report shows what was recorded, not what is physically in front of you:

  • An item closed by its case was not scanned itself. The app assumes it is inside the case it was registered in, and says so in the report.
  • An item marked by hand – a code typed in, or a gap closed as "Found it" – is your statement, not a scan.
  • Scanning itself depends on the printed label, the lighting, the distance and the device.

The app helps you notice what's missing. It doesn't replace checking the gear itself.

What we don't control

  • Your data and backups. Everything is stored only on your device. If the device is lost, or the app is deleted without a backup, we cannot recover anything. Export a full backup from Settings from time to time.
  • Other apps. Sharing, printing and saving files go through the operating system and other apps.
