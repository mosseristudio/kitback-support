Kitback support

Kitback tracks production gear: scan everything on the way out and on the way back, and the app tells you what didn't come home. Everything stays on your phone.

Contact

A question, a problem or an idea: write to mosseri.studio@gmail.com. We read every message, and answer in English or Hebrew.

Common questions

How do I get started?
Add your gear in Inventory, print labels and stick them on. Before a shoot, tap Head out on Home and scan everything on the way out. At the end, tap Scan back in and scan what came back. The report is ready as soon as you finish.

How do I print labels?
In Inventory, tap the QR icon. Pick the items and a size – case (50×30 mm) or cable (30×18 mm) – and tap Create PDF. Print it on a home printer at 100% scale.

Can I scan without the camera?
Yes. On the scan screen, tap Enter code by hand and type the code printed under the QR. Upper or lower case doesn't matter.

What does "Closed by its case" mean?
Scanning a case closes everything registered inside it, so you don't have to scan each lens. The report lists those items separately, so you know what you relied on. Anything marked Always scan individually is never closed this way.

An item shows as missing, but I have it.
Open the trip in Trips, tap Resolve next to the item and choose Found it. An open gap stays on Home until it's closed, even after the trip ends.

Can my producer see a trip?
Open the trip's report and tap the share icon (Export summary), then send the file. On their phone it opens from Trips → Open a received summary. It's read-only: nothing on their phone changes.

Where is my data kept?
Only on your device, and normally in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google). We don't see it. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone. How do I move my gear?
On the old phone: Settings → Export full backup, and save the file. On the new phone: Settings → Restore from a file. Restoring replaces whatever is already on the new phone.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data. The app shows what will be removed and offers a backup first.
